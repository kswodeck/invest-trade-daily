## [06:17 ET] NEWS — the proposal is the CRUSH rule, and a federal court already vacated its two core mechanisms
This is the most important thing I have found today. The 2026-11-18 managed-care selloff was caused by a *proposed* rule whose two operative provisions were struck down in court fourteen months ago on grounds the proposal does not cure.
- **What it is**: CMS's **CRUSH** proposed rule (Comprehensive Regulations to Uncover Suspicious Healthcare). It would (a) **extrapolate RADV audit findings starting with payment year 2018**, (b) codify the **absence of a fee-for-service (FFS) adjuster**, and (c) let CMS determine the sampling methodology itself.
- **Scale CMS itself puts on it**: roughly **$9B per payment year**, about **$90B over a decade**. CMS notes it **has not yet been assessed by the CMS Actuary** — i.e. the agency's own headline number is unvetted.
- **Comments are due 2027-01-19.** Nothing can be finalized before that date, and in practice a final rule follows months later. There is no mechanism by which this costs anyone a dollar inside a swing horizon.
- **The precedent, and this is the thesis**: on **2025-09-25 the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Texas vacated the 2023 RADV final rule**, holding that eliminating the FFS Adjuster was **arbitrary and capricious** and that applying the rule to prior payment years was **impermissibly retroactive**. CRUSH proposes to do both of those things again — extrapolate from PY2018 and codify no FFS adjuster.
- sources: https://www.risehealth.org/insights-articles/article/cms-crush-proposed-rule-medicare-advantage/ | https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=fcf81fc6-bf42-4261-a1f0-c8d1fa399792 | https://www.hfma.org/legal-and-regulatory-compliance/cms-crush-proposed-rule/ | https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/client-alerts/20251002-decision-vacating-cms-rule-governing-radv-audits-has-important-implications-for-medicare-advantage-organizations | https://www.groom.com/resources/court-rules-that-cms-cannot-extrapolate-medicare-advantage-risk-adjustment-audit-results/
